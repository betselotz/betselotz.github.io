Hello, I'm Betselot

Decoding pathogen genomes to strengthen public health in Ethiopia.

I am a bioinformatician at the Ethiopian Public Health Institute (EPHI), applying genomics and bioinformatics across bacterial and viral pathogens — from drug-resistant tuberculosis to SARS-CoV-2 and Ethiopia's first Marburg virus outbreak.

I joined EPHI in 2018 after graduating in Medical Laboratory Sciences from Addis Ababa University, and grew from laboratory diagnostics into genomics. During the COVID-19 pandemic I was among the first to work in EPHI's newly established SARS-CoV-2 lab and helped set up similar labs across regions of Ethiopia. In 2025 I contributed to sequencing and genomic analysis of Ethiopia's first Marburg virus outbreak, providing genomic confirmation for the national response within 24 hours.

I am a PhD candidate in Bioinformatics at Heinrich-Heine University Düsseldorf through the EpiGen Ethiopia consortium, and hold an MSc in Bioinformatics (2025) and an MSc in Medical Microbiology (2023) from Addis Ababa University. My work spans outbreak genomics, antimicrobial resistance, transmission analysis, wastewater surveillance and building bioinformatics capacity at EPHI.

Research

What I work on

Outbreak genomics

Rapid sequencing and genomic analysis of emerging viral threats — including Ethiopia's first Marburg virus outbreak and SARS-CoV-2 — to inform public health response.

Antimicrobial resistance

Genomic detection of drug resistance in bacterial pathogens, including whole-genome-sequencing-based surveillance of drug-resistant and XDR tuberculosis.

Phylogenomics & transmission

Lineage typing, phylogenetics and transmission dynamics of bacterial and viral pathogens circulating in Ethiopia.

Surveillance & capacity building

Wastewater and integrated genomic surveillance, reproducible pipelines, and training to build a robust bioinformatics facility at EPHI.
